Expected Solution: The 4 Core AI Screening Modules

Automated Pipeline
1 Module 1: OCR Extraction

Extracts fields from Passport (MRZ Lines 1 & 2, Doc No, Name, DOB, Expiry, Nationality, Gender), Visa (Number, Type, Issue/Validity, Country, Visa Stamp), National ID, Driving License, and Permit Documents.

2 Module 2: Document Validation

Validates rules & databases: checks expiration dates, modified DOB plausibility, calculates ICAO 9303 MRZ 7-3-1 algorithmic checksums, and cross-checks Border & Interpol Watchlists.

3 Module 3: Tampering Detection

Detects altered photographs, modified dates of birth, tampered visa stamps, and localized edge/font splicing using Laplacian variance and block density discontinuity.

4 Module 4: Face Detection & Biometrics

Detects credential portrait photo, captures live traveler camera feed, and performs 1:1 facial biometric matching to prevent identity impersonation.
