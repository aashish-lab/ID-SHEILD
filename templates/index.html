Terms of Service & Operational Protocols

1. Authorization: Access to ID SHIELD is restricted to authorized border security personnel, immigration agents, and credential verification officers.

2. Algorithmic Advisory: Triage ratings (CLEAR FOR ENTRY, SECONDARY INSPECTION, DETAIN & INVESTIGATE) are algorithmic decision-support tools. All final travel decisions rest with the primary inspecting officer.

3. Compliance: All MRZ extractions adhere to ICAO Document 9303 specifications. Unauthorized duplication, dissemination, or reverse-engineering is strictly prohibited.

4. Auditability: Every verification query is immutably logged with an audit hash and timestamp in compliance with border control directives.

Data Privacy & PII Handling Policy

1. PII Protection: Traveler biometric identifiers and Machine Readable Zone records are processed in memory and encrypted at rest in accordance with standard PII guidelines.

2. Data Retention: Inspection logs are maintained exclusively for border security audit trails and forensic dispute resolution.

3. Biometric Vectors: Live facial feature representations are utilized solely for instantaneous 1:1 cross-comparison against the credential portrait and are not transferred to commercial third parties.
